# Research: DJ learning and practice software

Compiled 2026-09-25 from web search. Reddit and most vendor sites were unreachable from the
research container; complaints come from Trustpilot, store snippets and review sites.

## Mainstream DJ software with learning features

- **rekordbox** — free core, Pro subscriptions. First-connection tutorial for DDJ-FLX2; mobile Lite mode inherits WeDJ tutorials and pop-hints; assist features Phrase Sync and Transition FX do the mix for you; CONNECT hub links Pete Tong DJ Academy and Point Blank. No scoring, no drills. Borrow: hardware-detected onboarding, assists you can turn off. [FLX2 tutorial](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/flx2-getting-started/), [DDJ-200 tutorials](https://support.pioneerdj.com/hc/en-us/articles/9443089194521-DDJ-200-and-WeDJ-Tutorials-GETTING-STARTED), [CONNECT](https://rekordbox.com/en/connect/)
- **Serato DJ Lite/Pro** — Practice Mode mixes two decks with mouse/keyboard and a practice crossfader; video tutorials only. [Practice mode](https://support.serato.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001274635-Practice-mode)
- **Traktor Pro 4 / Traktor Play** — Play is free, bundled with FLX2/FLX4, guided video. [Traktor Play](https://mixdownmag.com.au/news/native-instruments-launches-traktor-play-for-beginner-djs/)
- **Algoriddim djay Pro** — "djay School" video lessons by pro DJs, first episode free; supports DDJ-FLX4. [djay School](https://www.algoriddim.com/djschool), [FLX4 support](https://www.pioneerdj.com/en-us/news/2023/ddj-flx4-officially-supports-djay/)
- **VirtualDJ** — in-app tutorials tab. [manual](https://virtualdj.com/manuals/virtualdj/settings/tutorials.html)
- **Mixxx** — open source; manual-based learning; community FLX4 mapping is a MIDI reference. [manual](https://manual.mixxx.org/2.3/en/chapters/getting_started.html), [FLX4 mapping thread](https://mixxx.discourse.group/t/pioneer-ddj-flx4/26553)
- **edjing Mix** — mobile DJ School with step-by-step lessons and exercises on your own tracks. [Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.edjing.edjingdjturntable)
- **Cross DJ**, **DJ.Studio** — in-app tips; DJ.Studio's colour-coded key compatibility teaches harmonic mixing. [DJ.Studio review](https://altwire.net/dj-studio-3-0-review-discount-code)
- **Hercules DJUCED + DJControl Inpulse** — the only mainstream product with hardware beatmatch feedback: LED arrows by the pitch fader and jog show which way to move; green when locked; guide can be disabled. DJ Academy videos in-app; Intelligent Music Assistant. Borrow: directional real-time cues that fade out as you improve. [review](https://www.recordcase.de/en/hercules-djcontrol-inpulse-500-the-versatile-dj-controller-for-beginners-and-advanced-users)

## Dedicated beatmatch and DJ trainers

- **Beatmatch PRO** — free iOS/Android; match tempo by ear on screen, each attempt scored /100; Daily Challenge (same two tracks for everyone, "Wordle for DJing") with public leaderboard; practice filters by genre. Complaint: practice attempts later capped. Borrow: daily shared challenge, percent score on a continuous skill. [DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/news/free-app-helps-djs-practice-beatmatching-ear-daily-challenges), [site](https://www.beatmatch.pro/about)
- **beatmatch-trainer** (open-source PWA) — two decks with a hidden tempo offset, ±8/16% pitch and nudge; reveals BPM error; "strobe-dot" row that drifts at your error rate; "Beat Focus" low-pass at 220 Hz; states tempo error is audible as a flam past ~20 ms. [GitHub](https://github.com/marjez/beatmatch-trainer)
- **Beatmatching Trainer** (Android) — which beat is faster; speed-weighted points and streak bonus. [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/beatmatching-trainer/com.jonathanhook.beatmatchtrainer)
- **SF DJ Academy BeatMatch Trainer**, **Tempo Practice** (ahead/behind colour with tolerance band), **DJ Masterclass** (lessons, quizzes, no hardware), **The DJ Coach** (free 5/7-day classes with Pioneer; daily 20–30 min sessions ending in a quiz and exercise; submit a mix for human feedback). [DJ Coach](https://edmidentity.com/2020/09/29/pioneer-dj-free-courses-dj-coach/)

## Scratch trainers

TableBeats, Lino, Looper Scratch Practice: loop libraries with pitch control, no scoring. Mixfader: Bluetooth crossfader with tutorials, curve and cut-in settings (borrow as drill parameters). [Mixfader](https://thenextweb.com/news/wireless-mixfader-for-djs-now-up-for-pre-order-scratch-tutorial-app-on-the-way)

## Pad / finger-drumming trainers

- **Melodics** — desktop + iPad; pads, keys, e-drums. Plug-and-play list includes many Pioneer DDJs (200, SB/SB2/SB3, SP1, SR/SR2, SX/SX2/SX3, SZ/SZ2, RB/RR/RX/RZ, XP1/XP2, DJM-S9, DJS-1000); other devices via manual mapping; FLX4 not listed. Past Pioneer partnership gave free lessons by serial number. Note highway with per-hit colours, Performance vs Practice, Wait Mode, Auto-BPM, metronome; accuracy % and 1–3 stars; Daily Goal 5 min, streaks, trophies, practice calendar. Free = 60 lessons and 5 min performance/day; Standard ~$12.49/mo; Premium adds song lessons. Complaints: price, MIDI required, billing disputes; no DJ-specific skills. [supported instruments](https://support.melodics.com/en/articles/10763434-supported-instruments), [Pioneer partnership](https://idmmag.com/tech/tech_news/melodics-teams-pioneer-dj/), [plans](https://melodics.com/plans), [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/melodics.com)

## Games

- **DJ Hero 1/2** — turntable controller, note highway with taps, direction-specific scratches, crossfader cuts, effects; multiplier to 4× reset on miss; Euphoria; DJH2 freestyle sections and star ratings. Criticism: skills did not transfer to real DJing. Franchise cancelled 2011. [DJ Hero 2](https://djhero.fandom.com/wiki/DJ_Hero_2), [review](https://www.digitaltrends.com/game-reviews/dj-hero-2-review/)
- **Fuser** (delisted 2022) — stem decks; bonus for downbeat drops; crowd requests with time pressure; crowd meter decays; freestyle 32-bar mix sharing. [NME tips](https://www.nme.com/features/gaming-features/fuser-tips-and-tricks-2823998)
- **DJ Life Simulator** (2026 Early Access, $19.99) — career sim with real USB/MIDI controllers (Pioneer plug-and-play), crowd NPCs, academy lessons, harmonic mixing, track import. Direct competitor to watch; feedback is crowd simulation, not ms scoring. [site](https://djlifesimulator.com/), [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/explore-the-secrets-behind-dj-work-with-this-game-built-on-real-industry-knowledge)
- **Spin Rhythm XD** — rhythm game with native MIDI DJ controller support (jog spins the wheel); Perfect+ hidden precision tier; calibration. [MIDI guide](https://www.spinrhythmgame.com/spin-rhythm-xd-midi-guide)
- **Tribe XR DJ Academy** (VR) — virtual CDJ-3000/DJM decks, technique "game mode" tutorials, Pete Tong lessons, live workshops, 5-day challenge; $9.99/mo, $79.99/yr, $199 lifetime; no real hardware input. [pricing](https://www.tribexr.com/pricing)
- **Vinyl Reality**, **Electronauts**, **Stage Tour** (2026, from GH/DJH devs, "not DJ Hero"). [Stage Tour](https://worthplaying.com/article/2026/2/26/news/149137-stage-tour-is-a-new-rhythm-game-by-guitar-herodj-hero-devs-coming-to-pc-and-consoles-in-fall-2026-trailer/)

## Course platforms

- **Crossfader** — controller-specific courses incl. DDJ-FLX4 (37 lessons); one-time £70–115; knowledge checks per lesson; Beginner/Improver stages; bundled practice tracks. [courses](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/online-dj-courses)
- **Digital DJ Tips** (~$497 one-time, community, monthly live classes), **Pete Tong DJ Academy** (from $13/mo, 6-level ladder, feedback on mixes, contests with labels), **Scratch DJ Academy**, **Point Blank**, **DJ Courses Online** ($19/mo). [PTDJA](https://www.petetong-djacademy.com/dj-course)
- **Ableton Learning Music / Learning Synths** — free browser lessons where each concept is taught by an embedded playable instrument: the model for "explain the why, then let them touch it". [Learning Synths](https://learningsynths.ableton.com/)

## Harmonic and mashup helpers

Mixed In Key (Camelot + energy levels), PulseDJ (next-track copilot), DJ.Studio. None score technique. [MIK guide](https://mixedinkey.com/harmonic-mixing-guide/)

## Common complaints across the market

Video-only learning that never checks you did the move; single-skill trainers; subscription fatigue and billing disputes; games that don't transfer or got delisted; paywalled first lesson; platform lock-in.

## Whitespace

No existing tool scores real fader, knob or jog movement against a target curve, and none delivers ms-level pad timing for DJ-specific drills on a DDJ-FLX4. Nearest: Melodics (pads only, FLX4 not listed), Hercules' LED guide (hardware-bound, binary), DJ Life Simulator (crowd sim).
