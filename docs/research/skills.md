## Tier 1 — Foundations

- **Counting beats/bars/phrases** — hear 4/4, count across 8/16/32-bar phrases; changes land on phrase boundaries. *CUE tap / pad.* *Score*: tap on beat 1 of each bar/phrase within ±40 ms. *All.* [WIKI Phrasing; [DJ.Studio](https://dj.studio/blog/phrasing-dj-mixing); [Mixxx wiki](https://github.com/mixxxdj/mixxx/wiki/Measures-Downbeats-Bars-And-Phrases)]
- **Headphone cueing** — audition the next track on one ear; cue mix knob left then blend. *Headphone CUE, MIX knob.* *Score*: correct button state + CUE set on the chosen downbeat. [[DDJT](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/mixing-in-your-headphones/), [Rane](https://support.rane.com/en/support/solutions/articles/69000858891-understanding-and-utilizing-the-headphone-cue)]
- **Setting cue points / hot cues on the downbeat** — *CUE, HOT CUE pads.* *Score*: cue within ±20 ms of grid beat 1; recall lands on beat. [[XF hot cues](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/hot-cues-complete-guide/)]
- **Gain staging** — TRIM so meters peak green/orange, never red. *TRIM, meters, MASTER.* *Score*: peak level held in a window for N bars. [[DJTT](https://djtechtools.com/2015/10/11/gain-staging-for-djs-staying-out-of-the-red/), [XF](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/setting-and-managing-your-levels/)]
- **Tempo matching (pitch fader)** — *TEMPO.* *Score*: reach target BPM ±0.1 within N s, no overshoot oscillation. [MXX; [Pirate](https://pirate.com/en/blog/how-to-beatmatch/)]
- **Phase alignment: jog nudge** — brief push on the jog edge, release. *Jog (pitch bend).* *Score*: impulse in the correct direction; phase error < 15 ms; no over-correction. [[Pirate](https://pirate.com/en/blog/how-to-beatmatch/), [Bangerz Army](https://bangerz-army.com/blog/mastering-dj-beat-matching-techniques-a-comprehensive-guide/)]
- **Phase alignment: tempo-fader ride** — nudge with the fader then return ("pitch riding"). *Score*: temporary offset then return within ±0.05%. [[Bangerz Army](https://bangerz-army.com/blog/mastering-dj-beat-matching-techniques-a-comprehensive-guide/)]
- **Sustained beatmatch by ear** — keep two tracks locked 16–64 bars. *Score*: drift < 20 ms over 32 bars; count corrections. [MXX]
- **SYNC as a tool** — press SYNC, still nudge; good beatgrids are the prerequisite. [[DDJT](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/5-tips-for-better-sync-mixing/), [DJTT beatgrids](https://djtechtools.com/2014/04/23/how-to-set-beatgrids-and-use-sync-properly-in-serato-dj/)]
- **Channel-fader blend** — B up over 8–16 bars, A down. *Score*: monotonic ramp starting and ending on phrase boundaries. [[DDJT five transitions](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/rock-the-dancefloor/five-basic-dj-transitions/), [NI](https://blog.native-instruments.com/dj-transitions/)]
- **Crossfader curve knowledge** — slow for blends, sharp for cuts and scratching. *Score*: correct setting for the drill type. [[XF](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/scratching-crossfader-curve/), [Serato](https://support.serato.com/hc/en-us/articles/223446728-Mixer)]
- **Filter awareness (CFX)** — left low-pass, right high-pass, centre off. *Score*: knob returns to detent. [[DJLearn](https://djlearn.de/en/how-to-use-filters-dj)]
- **Mic / announcements** — not scorable. [[DJ Revolution](https://www.thedjrevolution.com/how-to-engage-audiences-using-a-microphone/)]

## Tier 2 — Mixing

- **Phrase-matched mix in** — start incoming on the outgoing's phrase boundary. *Score*: PLAY within ±30 ms of phrase 1. *House/techno.* [WIKI Phrasing; [VIB](https://vibesdj.io/learn/techniques/phrase-mixing)]
- **Bass swap** — LOW down on A while LOW up on B; one bass at a time. *Score*: crossing within 1 beat–1 bar; never both > 50%. *House/tech house/techno.* [[Vibes](https://vibesdj.io/learn/techniques/eq-swapping), [DJ.Studio](https://dj.studio/blog/dj-eqmixing)]
- **Staggered / top-down EQ swap** — B enters with all bands cut; swap HI, MID, LOW over 8–16 bars. *Score*: ordered ramps ~4 bars each. *Techno/house.* [[Home DJ Studio](https://homedjstudio.com/dj-eqing/), [MG](https://www.mixgraph.io/learn/dj-transition-techniques)]
- **Gradual EQ morph** — 32–64-bar blends. *Trance/techno/prog.* [[The DJ Mixtape](https://thedjmixtape.com/how-to-use-dj-set/), [XF trance](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/how-to-dj-trance-music/)]
- **Filter mix-out (LPF/HPF)** — sweep CFX on outgoing over 4–8 bars, cut fader; variant with LPF out + HPF in. [[MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/news/eq-and-filter-tricks-for-djs), [Learningtodj](https://learningtodj.com/blog/practical-guide-to-dj-filters/)]
- **Outro-to-intro full-volume mix** — both faders full ≥16 bars, bass one at a time. *House/techno.* [[XF house](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/3-ways-to-mix-house-music/)]
- **Crossfader cut / drop mix on the one** — snap crossfader on the downbeat. *Score*: traverse < 100 ms landing ±30 ms. *Hip-hop/open format/trap.* [[DDJT dropmix](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/dropmix-like-a-pro-hip-hop-dj/), [VIB](https://vibesdj.io/learn/techniques/track-transition-techniques)]
- **Fader cuts / chops** — rhythmic on/off on 8ths/16ths. *Score*: pattern match ±25 ms. [[DDJT](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/fader-cut-fundamentals-free-lesson/), [XF 12 transitions](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/12-high-energy-dj-transitions/)]
- **Echo out** — Echo on the last beat of the phrase, pull fader; incoming over the tail. *BEAT FX ECHO + CH SELECT, fader/PLAY.* *Score*: FX on within the last beat, cut ≤ 1 beat later, PLAY on the next one. [[Serato echo](https://support.serato.com/hc/en-us/articles/202304784-How-to-do-echo-transitions-in-Serato-DJ), [DJ Gym](https://www.djgym.co.uk/post/5-ways-to-use-echo)]
- **Backspin / spinback out** — reverse jog on beat 4; cut before it restarts; Pad FX "Backspin" on FLX4. *Hip-hop, house.* [[DJingTips](https://www.djingtips.com/how-to-dj/backspins-stops-and-power-offs/), SER]
- **Brake out** — Pad FX "Braker" on the last beat; new track on the one. [SER; [DJ.Studio](https://dj.studio/blog/dj-crossfade-techniques)]
- **Reverb wash-out** — reverb on the last snare, let it tail. [[Home DJ Studio FX](https://homedjstudio.com/dj-sound-effects/)]
- **Loop the outgoing outro** — 4/8-bar loop ~45 s before the end. *IN/4BEAT, OUT, RELOOP.* *Score*: presses on bar boundaries ±30 ms; length ∈ {4,8,16} beats. *Techno* (Charlotte de Witte). [[Electronic Beats](https://www.electronicbeats.net/played-out-charlotte-de-witte/), [XF loops](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/dj-looping-guide/)]
- **Harmonic mixing (Camelot)** — same, ±1, or A↔B. *Score*: chosen key compatible. [MIK; [DJ.Studio Camelot](https://dj.studio/blog/camelot-wheel)]
- **Energy boost mix** — +2 Camelot (whole step) or +7 (semitone) during a percussive section. [[MIK energy](https://mixedinkey.com/workflows/change-energy-with-camelot-wheel/)]
- **Key shift / Master Tempo** — Key Shift ±1–2 semitones on a phrase. *KEY SHIFT pads.* [[XF key lock](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/key-lock/), SER]
- **Set structure / energy arc** — advisory, not scored. [[SetFlow](https://www.setflow.app/blog/dj-set-energy-flow), [ZIPDJ](https://www.zipdj.com/blog/how-to-structure-a-dj-set)]

## Tier 3 — Performance

- **Loop roll / halving build** — 4 → 2 → 1 → ½ → ¼ beats before the drop, exit on the one. *Score*: regular halving intervals, exit on the one. *EDM/house/techno.* [[DDJT loops](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/three-ways-to-use-loops-without-annoying-everyone/), [XF](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/12-high-energy-dj-transitions/)]
- **Drop swap** — build on A, cut to B's drop on the one. *EDM/bass/hip-hop.* [[VIB](https://vibesdj.io/learn/techniques/track-transition-techniques)]
- **Double drop** — both full volume so drops coincide; 32-bar overlap in DnB. *DnB (signature), dubstep.* [[XF DnB](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/how-to-mix-drum-bass-free-dj-tutorial/), [MG DnB](https://www.mixgraph.io/mixing-guide/drum-and-bass)]
- **Fisher-style pre-drop entry** — B starts 16 bars after A's last drop; kill A 2 bars before B's drop; 1-bar dips. *Tech house* (Fisher). [[Club Ready](https://www.clubreadydjschool.com/tribe-talk/mix-tips/how-to-dj-like-fisher)]
- **Breakdown swap** — B's beat under A's breakdown; A cut so B's drop lands. *Trance.* [[MG trance](https://www.mixgraph.io/mixing-guide/trance)]
- **Flanger build** — on for the final 16 beats, off on the drop. [[Thomann](https://www.thomann.de/blog/en/inspire/dj-effects-chasing-the-dramatic-drop/)]
- **Beat roll build** — 1/4 → 1/8 → 1/16 into the drop (Carl Cox: subtle rolls). *ROLL / Pad FX Repeater.* [[DJTT](https://djtechtools.com/2011/03/20/create-amazing-build-ups-beat-roll/), [XF Cox](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/carl-cox-mixing-techniques/)]
- **Filter + echo combo / Smart CFX** — one-knob combos; return to centre on the one. [[XF Smart Fader](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/ddj-flx4-smart-fader-tutorial/), FLX]
- **Smart Fader tempo transition** — crossfader auto-ramps BPM with echo; still cut on the phrase. [[XF](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/ddj-flx4-smart-fader-tutorial/)]
- **Beat jump navigation** — 4/8/16/32 beats to realign or skip intros. *BEAT JUMP pads.* [[DJTT](https://djtechtools.com/amp/2016/09/15/underused-dj-techniques-beat-jump/), [DDJT](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/dj-tips-tricks-mixing-serato-dj-beatless-intros/)]
- **Instant doubles + ½-beat jump** — same track both decks, B offset exactly ½ beat. [[XF](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/instant-doubles-routine-how-its-done/)]
- **Sampler drops** — one-shots on the downbeat; risers N bars before. *SAMPLER pads.* *Open format/EDM.* [[Product London](https://www.productlondon.com/dj-sampler-techniques/), [DDJT](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/how-to-add-a-sampler-to-your-dj-set-up/)]
- **Pad FX stabs** — hold Echo/Flanger/Reverb/Repeater whole beats; toggle Echo Out/Backspin/Braker/Rollout on the phrase end. [SER]
- **Censor** — Beat Jump pad 8 reverse on a lyric. *Open format.* [SER]
- **Acapella over instrumental** — key/BPM-matched, starts on the phrase one. [[DDJT](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/how-to-dj-with-acapellas/)]
- **Tempo bridging (half/double time)** — 87 ↔ 174, 70 ↔ 140; house 124 → ~87 via echo out → 174 layer. [[DDJT](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/how-to-mix-using-half-time-and-double-time-bpms/), [VIB](https://vibesdj.io/learn/techniques/auto-bpm-transition), PDJ]
- **Gradual tempo creep** — ~1–2% per track. [[DJ.Studio](https://dj.studio/blog/dj-tempo-change-techniques)]
- **Quick mixing / dropmix** — cut in on the one every 8–16 bars with hot cues. *Hip-hop/open format.* [[DDJT](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/dropmix-like-a-pro-hip-hop-dj/), [Mixcloud DJ88](https://www.mixcloud.com/blog/2026/04/13/a-guide-to-open-format-djing-with-dj88/)]
- **Power (block) mixing** — new track every 30–60 s, key blocks; Armin, Zedd, KSHMR, Skrillex named. *EDM/festival.* [[MIK power block](https://mixedinkey.com/harmonic-mixing-guide/power-block-dj-mixing-tutorial/)]

## Tier 4 — Advanced

- **Hot cue drumming** — cues on kick/snare/hat; replay patterns in time (DJ EZ, James Hype). *Score*: pad hits match the pattern grid ±20 ms. [[DJTT cue juggling](https://djtechtools.com/2008/01/03/controllerism-basics-cue-point-juggling/), [XF hip hop](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/how-to-mix-hip-hop/), [DJ.Studio](https://dj.studio/blog/dj-cue-points)]
- **Cue-point juggling across decks** — rhythmic triggers + crossfader. [[DJTT 4-song](https://djtechtools.com/2010/04/18/4-song-cue-point-juggle/)]
- **Word play** — hot cue a word on B mirroring the last word of A's phrase. *Hip-hop/open format.* [[XF](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/how-to-mix-hip-hop/)]
- **Tone play** — melody from pitched hot cues / Key Shift pads. [[XF tone play](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/free-tone-play-mixing-lesson/), [DJTT](https://djtechtools.com/2016/06/19/new-turntablism-trick-midi-mapped-tone-play-cue-points/)]
- **Stem swaps** — drop A's drums, keep B's; vocals of A over B; swap drums before melody. *Score*: toggles on phrase boundaries; never two drum stems > 1 bar. [SER Stems; RB; [StemSplit](https://stemsplit.io/blog/dj-stem-separation-guide)]
- **Live remixing** — loops + cues + stems + sampler. [[NI](https://blog.native-instruments.com/live-remixing/)]
- **Scratching, basic** — baby, scribble, drag, stab/forward, tear, chirp, transformer. *Score*: jog velocity profile + fader open/close count per stroke on grid. *Hip-hop.* [FAN; SS; [DJingPro](https://djingpro.com/learn-how-to-scratch/); [DDJT controller scratching](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/scratching-on-controllers-7-myths-busted/)]
- **Scratching, advanced** — flare (1/2/3-click), orbit, crab, twiddle, hydroplane. *Score*: exact click count per stroke; clicks < 60 ms. [WIKI Scratching; [DJTT orbit](https://djtechtools.com/2015/01/22/take-your-scratch-technique-into-orbit/); [DJ Times](https://www.djtimes.com/2021/08/itching-to-scratch-tutorial-2-click-flare-orbit/)]
- **Scratch routines / combos** — notated combos, melodic scratching (A-Trak). [[Mixmag](https://mixmag.net/feature/an-oral-history-a-traks-journey-with-turntables), [EDM.com](https://edm.com/gear-tech/a-trak-dj-notes-art-of-scratching)]
- **Beat juggling** — two copies; extend a beat by cutting between decks; strobing/chasing. [WIKI Beat juggling; [School of Scratch](https://schoolofscratch.com/beat-juggling/)]
- **Cue-point scratch** — voice a cue press like a scratch with the fader. [[DJTT](https://djtechtools.com/2011/02/27/the-cue-point-scratch-a-new-concept-in-digital-djing/)]

## Genre transition defaults

House/tech house: outro→intro, bass swap on phrase, 16–32 bars. Techno: 32–64-bar EQ blends, loop-out, filters. Trance: 32–64-bar blends, breakdown swap, harmonic strictness. DnB: 16–32-bar, double drops, cut lows. Dubstep (140 half-time): bass swaps, hard cuts, drop swaps, 70↔140. Hip-hop/open format: drop mix on the one, 8-bar, word play, fader cuts, backspins, tempo jumps. [[XF trance](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/how-to-dj-trance-music/), [MG DnB](https://www.mixgraph.io/mixing-guide/drum-and-bass), [DJ.Studio dubstep](https://dj.studio/blog/bpm-for-dubstep), [PDJ genres](https://blog.pioneerdj.com/djtips/we-uncover-the-mixing-techniques-behind-every-major-genre/)]

## Drill primitives (scoring engine backlog)

1. **TAP** — discrete press at a target beat. Score: |Δt| with tiers.
2. **RAMP** — continuous move A→B over N bars. Score: tracking error, start/end on phrase.
3. **HOLD** — value/state held for N beats.
4. **CROSS** — two mirrored ramps with an exclusivity constraint (bass swap).
5. **CUT** — near-instant traverse landing on the beat.
6. **ALTERNATE** — on/off pattern on a subdivision grid with click count.
7. **JOG NUDGE** — impulse sign/magnitude vs phase error; converge without overshoot.
8. **JOG STROKE** — velocity-profile shape per stroke (baby, scribble, drag, tear, backspin, brake) combined with ALTERNATE for chirp/flare/orbit.
9. **STEP** — parameter halves/doubles on bar boundaries then exits on the one.
10. **SELECT** — discrete choice validated by rule (Camelot, curve, CH SELECT, key shift, BPM ratio).
11. **SEQUENCE / COMBO** — ordered chain with timing constraints (echo out, drop swap, routines, signature moves).
12. **PLAN** — advisory only (set energy arc, mic).
