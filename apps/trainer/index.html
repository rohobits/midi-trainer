Chrome on macOS with the controller on USB; rekordbox can stay open. Play a track on deck A, set BPM to match, then hit Play A (or Space) on a phrase downbeat. Run "Map controls" once so the trainer knows your pads and faders.

Map controls

Click a control, then touch it on the hardware. Buttons register on press; knobs and faders on any move. Map only what you use; unmapped lanes are dimmed and their drills are marked unavailable.
