Chrome on your Mac with the FLX4 on USB; rekordbox can stay open. Play a track on deck A, set BPM to match, then hit Play A (or Space) exactly on a phrase downbeat — the drill clock starts from that press. Run "Map controls" once so the trainer knows your pads and faders.

Map controls

Click a control, then touch it on the FLX4. Buttons register on press; knobs and faders on any move. EQ and filter knobs: leave them at centre after mapping. Map only what you use; unmapped lanes are skipped in drills.
